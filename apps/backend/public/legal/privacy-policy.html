Privacy Policy

2. Location sharing during SOS and Trust Circle

This is opt-in and user-initiated. Doondo never shares your live location with anyone unless you start a shift check-in or trigger an SOS alert.

When you check in to a shift, or trigger an SOS alert, your live location and basic profile info (name, photo) are shared with:

  • Up to 3 people you've personally added as your Trust Circle, and
  • For SOS specifically, the 2 nearest verified Doondo users who've opted in as peer responders.

This sharing stops once the shift ends or the SOS alert is resolved. Doondo's SOS feature is a community safety layer, not a substitute for emergency services — in a real emergency, always contact local police or medical services directly.

3. Payments and Doondo Collect

Doondo Collect lets a worker generate a QR/pay-link so an employer can pay them, with Doondo recording the transaction for earnings tracking. Doondo does not yet route real money through a licensed payment aggregator — collections, bank verification, and payouts are currently simulated for testing the flow end-to-end, and no real funds move through Doondo's systems today. When a licensed payment aggregator (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) is switched on, this policy will be updated before any real money movement begins, and that provider's own privacy terms will also apply to the transaction.

4. Who we share information with

  • Other Doondo users — your profile, ratings, and (if you choose to share it) live location during a shift or SOS, as described above.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: our database host, our push-notification delivery service (Expo), and our SMS/OTP provider for phone verification. They only receive what's needed to perform that function and aren't permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Law enforcement — only when legally required (a valid court order, subpoena, or similar legal process).

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Chat and application history is kept for up to 24 months after the relevant conversation or application closes, to support dispute resolution (e.g. a payment or safety dispute), after which it is deleted or anonymised. If you delete your account, we remove your profile, photos, and contact-matching data within 30 days, except where we're required to retain limited records for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Deleting your account

You can request account deletion at any time from Profile → Account & settings → Delete account in the app, or by emailing us (see Contact below). We'll confirm once it's processed.

7. Your choices

  • Location, camera, microphone, and contacts permissions can be turned off at any time in your phone's system settings — the relevant Doondo feature will simply be unavailable until you turn it back on.
  • Notification preferences (jobs, applications, messages, ratings, referrals) can be toggled individually in the app.
  • You can request a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by contacting us.

8. Children

Doondo is intended for users 18 years and older, in line with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard one-way hashing, never in plain text. Data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Bank account numbers are masked everywhere they're displayed in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: support@doondo.app
